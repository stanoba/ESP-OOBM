Supports automatic DTR/RTS flashing • Live serial monitor directly in browser

🚀 Quick Flashing Steps

  1. Plug in Dongle: Insert your ESP-OOBM USB dongle into your computer's USB port.
  2. Click Connect: Press the green Connect & Flash ESP-OOBM button above.
  3. Select Serial Port: Choose the serial port corresponding to the CH343P / USB CDC device.
  4. Install & Erase: Select Install ESP-OOBM (check Erase Device on first flash) and wait ~30 seconds for completion.

🔄 Wireless OTA Firmware Update (For Existing Devices)

If you already have ESP-OOBM installed, you can update wirelessly without USB:

Download OTA Firmware (.bin)
  1. Open your device Web Portal at http://<device-ip>/update.
  2. Select the downloaded esp-oobm-firmware.bin and click Update Firmware.
  3. Device will flash the update into the inactive partition, reboot, and verify the new version seamlessly.
